The boundary

Air Fryer Math keeps labeled-norm arithmetic only. Basket areas, per-serving spacing and minutes are labeled norms, not measurements of your fryer, your food or your recipe - real results depend on the fryer's wattage, how crowded the layer is and how often you shake. Follow the fryer's manual and the recipe over anything this page says, and let a thermometer call the doneness.
