The boundary

Air Fryer Math keeps labeled-norm arithmetic only. Basket areas, per-serving spacing and minutes are labeled norms, not measurements of your fryer, your food or your recipe - real results depend on the fryer's wattage, how crowded the layer is and how often you shake. Follow the fryer's manual and the recipe over anything this page says, and let a thermometer call the doneness.

Quarts measure a pile, and nothing in an air fryer cooks in a pile. The honest unit is the single layer: the square inches the hot air can actually see. Once the layer is full, the rest of the recipe's servings join a queue, and the recipe's minutes were never written for a queue. Air Fryer Math does the honest arithmetic between the basket, the servings and the clock.

Three panels. The layer truth turns basket inches into honest layer area. The batch truth counts servings per layer and the batches behind them. The time truth prices the batches plus the preheat.
